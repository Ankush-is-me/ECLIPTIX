Casting Guide

Hot Wheels terms explained simply

Mainlines

The standard carded cars released every year. Good for pocket runs or desk displays.

Redlines

Vintage cars from 1968–1977, identified by the red line on the tire sidewalls.

Real Riders

Cars with real rubber tires on multi-piece wheels instead of plastic ones.

Super Treasure Hunts (STH)

Rare cars mixed into regular cases, with Spectraflame-style paint and rubber tires.

About ECLIPTIX

ECLIPTIX is a young, collector-focused die-cast sourcing business in Vadodara, Gujarat. We source Hot Wheels cars, check card condition, and deal with buyers directly so collecting is less guesswork.

We also make and sell Hot Wheels themed bag tags, for anyone who wants car culture on a backpack, keys or travel kit.
